lichtung
2026 · music & sound for contemporary dance
Lichtung is a contemporary dance work directed and choreographed by Raquel Castelló Rodrigo.

Starting from Castelló's artistic concept, Lucía Broto developed the musical direction and created the music and sound design for the piece.

The music combines a live ensemble of cello, flute, electric guitar and voice with electronics, creating a dialogue between acoustic performance and processed sound throughout the choreographic work.
